Master Specification Sheet

Brand Identity Guidelines

Bridging rugged industrial techwear aesthetics with functional, high-durability civic micro-station design. Standards for color, typography, and geometry.

01. Philosophy

Tone & Naming Protocols

The brand language is rooted in industrial reliability, modular efficiency, and technical precision. Every touchpoint—from hardware enclosures and apparel tags to digital dashboards—should communicate safety, durability, and open-source accessibility without relying on ornate or decorative motifs.

Directive: Avoid any religious, overly radical, or ambiguous phrasing. Keep nomenclature strictly functional and operational (e.g., Frames, Hulls, Cores).

03. Typography

Hierarchy & Typefaces

Primary Technical Face

JetBrains Mono / Space Mono

Used for asset tags, diagnostic readouts, spec callouts, and serial numbers.

Secondary Wayfinding Face

Inter / Roboto

Used for instructions, headers, and UI navigation.

Headers: Uppercase only, tracked out (letter-spacing increased by 5-10%).

Body Copy: Left-aligned, never justified. Minimum 1.25 line height.

04. Logo Geometry

Core Bracket Motif
